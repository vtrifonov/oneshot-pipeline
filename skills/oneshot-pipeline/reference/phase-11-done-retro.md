### Retro — after ten comparable completed runs, or when the user asks
- Compare runs within repository, comparable risk/size, policy version, and measurement coverage. Separate cancelled/blocked from ready runs and waiting time from reviewer execution where timestamps support it. Historical CSV rows without versioned artifacts are legacy observations; never infer missing per-lens data.
- Assess unique accepted BLOCKER/MAJOR contribution per lens, overlapping discoveries, rejected/duplicate findings, recheck churn, token/time data from `step-metrics.csv` (per phase, per agent, per inline step; compare `output` and `cache_write` before `total_tokens`, which is dominated by cache reads), panel elapsed time, PR-to-ready time, and later escapes. Inspect finding evidence; raw volume and parallel speed do not establish value. Concurrent shared discoveries do not become unique contributions by arrival order.
- Review lane-trigger yield separately from reviewer-count yield. High no-edit closure rates warrant sampling dispositions, not automatic claims of over-reporting. Zero no-change closes does not prove MINORs were fixed improperly. Later bugs are evidence, not the only reason to correct a demonstrably faulty trigger.
- Populate `defects_90d` only for runs at least 90 days old after a completed follow-up, recording traced bug/revert evidence and check date in the summary. Zero means checked. Ignore premature historical zeros in 90-day comparisons; retain original rows rather than silently rewriting history. Earlier escapes can be linked in the ledger immediately.
- Keep/change reviewer scopes based on accepted unique contribution, cost/churn, and escapes across comparable runs. After trial, document decision and limits; do not auto-demote a security gate because a small sample has no findings. Back up previous skill and record before/after plus rationale in local changelog when changing contract.
